# ChemKit Brand Guidelines (Monogram Catalyst Identity)

> Official brand identity documentation for ChemKit: The interactive chemistry toolkit for incoming STEM and chemistry students.

---

## 1. The Brand Identity
- **Concept**: **The Monogram Catalyst (`Ck`)**. A bold lowercase `c` and `k` bonded like an organic molecule. The `c` creates an orbital meniscus bowl cradling an emerald catalyst core, while the `k` ascender acts as a test-tube stem branching into a 45° molecular reaction node.
- **Tone**: Fun, helpful, approachable, modern, empowering. Designed to demystify chemistry for high school and collegiate STEM students.
- **Asset Hierarchy**:
  - `chemkit-horizontal.svg`: Primary horizontal lockup for headers, navigation, and banners.
  - `chemkit-stacked.svg`: Square stacked lockup for merchandise, packaging, and hero cards.
  - `chemkit-symbol.svg`: Standalone symbol for cards, avatars, and app badges.
  - `chemkit-symbol-small.svg`: Heavy-geometry cut for 16px and 32px favicons.

---

## 2. Clear Space & Proportions
- Keep an exclusion zone of **1 × $X$** around all edges of the mark, where $X$ equals the diameter of the emerald catalyst node (32px relative to 256px canvas).
- No UI element, text, or border should infringe upon this perimeter.

## 4. Color Palette

### 60:30:10 Scientific Contrast System
| Role | Name | HEX | RGB | Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **10% Accent** | **Catalyst Emerald** | `#00C853` | `rgb(0, 200, 83)` | Reaction node, primary CTAs, active highlights |
| **30% Structure** | **Near Black (Ink)** | `#111111` | `rgb(17, 17, 17)` | Monogram strokes, primary typography, borders |
| **60% Canvas (Light)** | **Pure White (Bg)** | `#FFFFFF` | `rgb(255, 255, 255)` | Page background, cards, high-contrast canvas |
| **60% Canvas (Dark)** | **Deep Void (Bg Dark)** | `#0C0D0E` | `rgb(12, 13, 14)` | Dark mode background |
| **Surface (Dark)** | **Dark Charcoal** | `#1E1E1E` | `rgb(30, 30, 30)` | Dark mode cards and UI surfaces |
| **Supporting Tint** | **Soft Mint** | `#D9F2E6` | `rgb(217, 242, 230)` | Badges, hover glows, secondary pill fills |

---

## 5. Typography
- **Logotype & Display**: *Archivo* / *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Weight: 900 Black).
- **Body & Numerical Values**: *Inter* (Weight: 400 Regular, 600 SemiBold, 700 Bold).
- **Formulas & Mono Data**: *JetBrains Mono* / *SF Mono* (Weight: 600, 700).

---

## 6. Logo Misuse Rules
1. **Never skew, rotate, or stretch** the monogram.
2. **Never alter the 45° molecular branch angle** of the `k`.
3. **Never separate the droplet core** from inside the `c` bowl.
4. **Never recolor outside approved palette** (Emerald `#00C853`, Ink `#111111`, White `#FFFFFF`).
5. **Never add drop shadows, outer glows, or 3D extrusions** to vector master marks.
