# ChemKit Brand Guidelines (v2.0 Redesign)

## 1. Brand Concept: The Catalyst Hexagon
The ChemKit brand identity departs completely from textbook orbiting-atom clichés. It is built upon the **benzene hexagon** — organic chemistry's foundational geometry.

- **The 'C' Facet (Chem / Carbon)**: The three left hexagonal vertices at 30, 60, and 120-degree angles carve out a crisp capital 'C'.
- **The 'K' Spine and Arms (Kit / Kinetic)**: A vertical central spine paired with rightward diagonal bonds at exactly 30 and 330 degrees forms the letter 'K'.
- **The Catalytic Prism**: The central node at coordinate (128, 128) denotes catalytic energy, transformation, and computational precision.

## 3. Typography
- **Display**: Space Grotesk (700 weight for headings, 600 weight for subheads).
- **Body / Interface**: Inter (400 regular, 500 medium, 600 semibold).
- **Technical / Formulas**: JetBrains Mono (molar masses, numbers, balance tables).
